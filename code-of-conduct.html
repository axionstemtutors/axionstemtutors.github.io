Tutor code of conduct

The promises every tutor makes before their first session.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Every tutor and their parent or guardian signs this code before the first session.

Be kind and respectful

  • Treat every student and family with patience and respect.
  • Encourage questions. Never mock or pressure a student.
  • Explain, don't just give answers.

Keep contact on our platform

  • Use first names only.
  • Do not share your phone number, social media, address, or any personal details, and do not ask students for theirs.
  • Do not message students or families privately, or contact them outside our platform.
  • No gifts, no meeting in person.

Run safe sessions

  • Join from a quiet, appropriate place, and dress as you would for school.
  • Stay on the lesson. Do not discuss personal or inappropriate topics.
  • Do not record sessions on your own device, or share session links or content.
  • If something feels uncomfortable or unsafe, end the session and tell our team right away.

Be reliable and honest

  • Show up on time, or cancel as early as you can and request a substitute.
  • Report your hours honestly.
  • Follow the lessons and guidance from our team.

If the code is broken

We may pause or end a tutor's participation for breaking this code. Serious concerns are reported to the proper authorities where the law requires.

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service for grades 2-6.

This site does not collect any information from children.
