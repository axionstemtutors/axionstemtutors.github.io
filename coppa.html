Notice to parents

Our children's privacy practices, in plain language.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) protects children under 13. Most of our students are under 13, so we treat all students this way.

Parents are in charge

Children do not create accounts. A parent or guardian creates the account, adds the child, and gives consent. We will ask for your verifiable consent before collecting any personal information from your child.

What we collect from a child, with your consent

  • A first name or nickname and a grade level
  • Subjects of interest
  • Participation in sessions, which can include voice, and video if the camera is on, and any work shared on screen
  • Recordings of sessions, described in our recording policy

We do not ask for last names, home addresses, school names, photos, or birthdates, and we do not let children message anyone outside a session.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell children's information or use it for advertising.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking tools where children use our service.
  • We do not post a child's name, image, or work publicly without separate written permission from a parent.

Who may see it

The student's tutor, our authorized adult team members, and service providers that help us run sessions (such as our video platform). We require them to protect the information.

Your rights

  • Review the information we have about your child
  • Ask us to delete it
  • Refuse any further collection and withdraw consent at any time

To do any of these, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request." If you withdraw consent, your child will no longer be able to join sessions.
