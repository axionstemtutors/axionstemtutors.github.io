Terms of use

The ground rules for using our site and sessions.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Our service

Axion STEM Tutors offers free tutoring in math, science, and computer science for grades 2-6, taught by trained teen volunteers. By using our website or services, you agree to these terms.

Parents and guardians

  • You must be a parent or legal guardian to create an account for a child.
  • You are responsible for your child's participation and for supervising your child during sessions, and you are welcome to sit in.
  • You agree to give accurate information and to follow our code of conduct and safety rules.

Tutors

Our tutors are volunteers, not certified teachers. Tutoring supports, but does not replace, school instruction. We cannot guarantee any particular academic result.

Rules of use

  • Be respectful and kind to everyone.
  • Do not share session links, record sessions yourself, or share session content.
  • Do not contact tutors or students outside our platform.
  • Do not misuse our website or try to access accounts that are not yours.

Our right to act

We may pause, decline, or end participation for anyone who breaks these terms or our safety rules, or if we cannot offer a session. Because the service is free and volunteer-run, sessions may be limited, rescheduled, or canceled.

Limits of liability

The service is provided as is, to the fullest extent allowed by law. Axion STEM Tutors and its volunteers are not liable for indirect or incidental harm from using the service.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will post the new date here. Questions? Use our contact form. These terms are governed by the laws of the state where Axion STEM Tutors is organized.

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service for grades 2-6.

This site does not collect any information from children.
