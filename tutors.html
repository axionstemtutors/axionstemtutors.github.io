For tutors

Teach younger students, build skills, and earn verified volunteer hours.

Sessions haven't started yet. Tutor interest forms open soon.

Who can tutor

  • Students 14 and older who are strong in Math, Science, or Computer Science.
  • A parent or guardian must approve your application.

How you become a tutor

  • Apply and share a reference from a teacher or counselor.
  • Meet with us for a short interview.
  • Complete training in teaching younger students, child safety, and how our platform works.
  • Sign our code of conduct.

How scheduling works

  • Once a month, you tell us when you're free for the following month. Skipping a month is fine.
  • Each session is 80 minutes, online.
  • If something comes up, you can request a substitute.

What you get

  • Verified volunteer hours, with a certificate you can use for school and applications.
  • Real teaching experience in a subject you love.

Ground rules

  • Keep all contact with students and families on our platform.
  • Use first names only.
  • You are never alone with a problem. Tell us right away if a session feels uncomfortable.

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service for grades 2-6.

This site does not collect any information from children.
