How it works

Parents stay in charge at every step.

  1. A parent signs up

    A parent or guardian creates the account and gives permission. Children never create their own accounts.

  2. We match a tutor

    Sessions are one-on-one. Occasionally two students share a tutor, and families are told ahead of time.

  3. The student joins an online session

    Sessions run over video with the camera optional. Parents are welcome to sit in at any time.

Teens 14 and older can teach

Volunteer tutors are trained before their first session, choose when they are free each month, and log verified volunteer hours. A parent or guardian approves every teen tutor.
