Child safety policy

Our commitments to every student and every tutor.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Who tutors

  • Tutors are teens age 14 and older. A parent or guardian approves each tutor.
  • Each tutor provides a reference from a teacher or counselor, completes an interview, finishes training, and signs our code of conduct.
  • Training covers child safety and boundaries, teaching younger students, and how to report concerns.

During sessions

  • Sessions are held on our platform with private, single-use links. Only registered participants are admitted.
  • Sessions are recorded, and adult team members review recordings, including random samples and anything reported.
  • Parents may sit in on any session, and our adult leaders may join any session.
  • Cameras are optional for students.
  • Sessions are one-on-one. Occasionally two students share a tutor, and families are told ahead of time.

Contact rules

  • Tutors and students never message each other privately and never contact each other outside our platform.
  • Everyone uses first names or nicknames only.
  • No gifts, no social media contact, and no meeting in person.

Reporting a concern

Anyone can report a concern at any time using our contact form and choosing "Safety concern." We take every report seriously, review it promptly, and keep a record. Tutors who feel uncomfortable can end a session at once and tell us.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911. Where the law requires us to report suspected abuse or neglect, we will report it to the proper authorities.

Protecting our teen tutors

Our tutors are minors too. They have the same reporting tools, the same rules about private contact, and our support if something goes wrong.

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service for grades 2-6.

This site does not collect any information from children.
