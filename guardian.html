About Axion STEM Tutors

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service run by volunteers. We offer online sessions in math, science, and computer science for students in grades 2-6. Tutors are volunteer high school students in grades 9-12. Each session lasts 80 minutes and is usually one-on-one. Sometimes two students in the same subject share a tutor.

Information we collect about your teen

  • Name, email address, grade, subjects they can tutor, and when they are available
  • Attendance and session length, used for volunteer hour records and certificates
  • Their voice, and video if their camera is on, during sessions, and recordings of sessions

What we promise

  • We do not sell your teen’s information or use it for advertising.
  • Tutors and students never message each other privately or contact each other outside our platform.
  • Tutors use first names or nicknames only, and their camera is optional. Audio-only is always allowed.
  • Parents of students may observe any session, and our adult leaders may join any session.
  • Anyone can report a concern at any time, and we respond promptly.

Your rights

You may at any time review the information we have about your teen, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent. If you withdraw consent, your teen can no longer tutor. Use our contact form and choose Privacy request.

See also our privacy policy, recording policy, child safety policy, and code of conduct. This form records how Axion works and what you agree to. It does not ask you to give up any legal rights.
