Recording policy

Why we record, who sees it, and how long we keep it.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Why we record

We record tutoring sessions for safety and quality: to keep students and tutors safe, to review concerns, and to help train tutors.

Consent

We record every session. Before a family joins, a parent or guardian must give consent, and so must the tutor's parent or guardian. If a family does not consent to recording, we cannot offer sessions to that family. When two students share a session, both families must consent.

What is recorded

Voice, video if cameras are on, shared screens and whiteboards, and chat. Cameras are optional for students, so a student may be heard but not seen.

Who can view recordings

Only authorized adult team members and reviewers. Recordings are never posted publicly, shared on social media, or used for marketing.

Storage and retention

  • Recordings are stored securely with restricted access.
  • We delete them after 30 days. We keep a recording longer only while a safety concern or legal matter involving it is being handled.
  • Recordings are never used for advertising.

Your requests

Parents may ask to review or delete their child's recordings by using our contact form and choosing "Privacy request." We may hold a recording briefly if it is part of a safety review.

No recording by others

Tutors, students, and parents may not record sessions on their own devices.

See also our privacy policy and notice to parents.

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service for grades 2-6.

This site does not collect any information from children.
