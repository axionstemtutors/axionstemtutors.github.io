Subjects

Math, science, and computer science for grades 2-6.

Math

Students build number sense and learn to explain their thinking, not just get answers.

Grades 2-3

  • Place value
  • Addition and subtraction
  • Intro to multiplication

Grades 4-5

  • Fractions and decimals
  • Multiplication and division
  • Word problems

Grade 6

  • Ratios
  • Order of operations
  • Intro to algebra

Science

We use questions and simple at-home experiments to explain how the world works.

Grades 2-3

  • Plants and animals
  • Weather
  • States of matter

Grades 4-5

  • Ecosystems
  • Energy and motion
  • Earth and space

Grade 6

  • Cells and living things
  • Forces
  • Climate and Earth systems

Computer science

Logic, patterns, and problem solving, with or without a computer screen.

Grades 2-3

  • Patterns and sequences
  • Step-by-step instructions
  • Intro to block coding

Grades 4-5

  • Loops and conditions
  • Debugging
  • Simple games

Grade 6

  • Variables and functions
  • Intro to text coding
  • Project building
Topics are examples. Tutors adapt to each student's grade level and school work.
