Step 1. Your consents

Read about how we handle information in our privacy policy, notice to parents, recording policy and child safety policy. This page records how Axion works and what you agree to. It does not ask you to give up any legal rights. You can change your choices later by returning to this page.

Step 2. A short video call

To make sure each account belongs to a real parent or guardian, a trained Axion volunteer will meet you for a short video call (about 10 minutes) before your child can be added. You do not need to share anything private on the call. Tell us how to reach you and when you are free.
