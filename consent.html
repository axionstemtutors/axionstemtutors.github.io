Before you agree: how Axion works

1. About Axion STEM Tutors

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service run by volunteers. We offer one-on-one online sessions in math, science, and computer science for students in grades 2-6. Our tutors are high school students in grades 9-12 who are trained, approved by their own parents, and supervised by our adult leadership. Each session lasts 80 minutes.

2. Information we collect about your child

With your consent, we collect only what we need to run sessions:

  • Your child\u2019s first name or a nickname you choose, and grade level
  • Subjects your child wants help with
  • Participation in sessions, including your child\u2019s voice, and video if the camera is on, plus anything shared on screen or in chat
  • Recordings of sessions (see 3.3 below)
  • Attendance and session length, used for volunteer hour records and totals

We do not collect last names, home addresses, school names, photos, or birthdates for children.

4. What we promise

  • We do not sell your child\u2019s information or use it for advertising.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party tracking tools where children use our service.
  • We collect as little information as possible, and we delete it on a schedule we publish.
  • Tutors and students never message each other privately or contact each other outside our platform.
  • Parents may sit in on any session, and our adult leaders may join any session.
  • Anyone can report a concern at any time, and we respond promptly.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Keep session links private, and do not share them.
  • Do not record sessions on your own devices, or share session content.
  • Make sure a parent or guardian is nearby during sessions, and available if needed.
  • Be respectful to our volunteer tutors, and follow our policies and tutor code of conduct.
  • Tell us right away if you have a concern, or if your contact information changes.

6. Your rights

You may, at any time, review the information we have about your child, ask us to correct or delete it, refuse further collection of your child\u2019s information, or withdraw any consent here. You can change your consents on this page, or use the contact form and choose Privacy request. If you withdraw a required consent, your child will no longer be able to join sessions.

7. Understanding the program

  • Tutoring supports school learning but does not replace it, and we cannot promise any particular result.
  • Sessions may be limited, rescheduled, or canceled because the service is free and run by volunteers.
  • We may pause or end participation for anyone who breaks our rules.

Step 1. Your consents (section 3)

Items marked required are needed for your child to take part. Read more in our privacy policy, notice to parents, recording policy and child safety policy. This page records how Axion works and what you agree to. It does not ask you to give up any legal rights. You can change your choices later by returning to this page.

Step 2. A short video call

To make sure each account belongs to a real parent or guardian, a trained Axion volunteer will meet you for a short video call (about 10 minutes) before your child can be added. You do not need to share anything private on the call. Tell us how to reach you and when you are free.
