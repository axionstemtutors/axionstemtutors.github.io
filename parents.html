For parents

What to expect, and how we keep your child safe.

Sessions haven't started yet. Interest forms open soon.

What a session looks like

  • Each session lasts 80 minutes and takes place online over video.
  • Sessions are one-on-one. Occasionally, two students of a similar grade share a tutor, and we tell you ahead of time. You can choose "one-on-one only," which may mean a longer wait.
  • Your child's camera is optional, and audio-only is always fine.

You stay in charge

  • A parent or guardian creates the account. Children never make their own.
  • We ask for your permission before collecting any information about your child, and we collect only what we need.
  • You can sit in on any session, and you can ask to see or delete your child's information at any time.

Safety

  • Our tutors are teen volunteers, 14 and older. Each is trained, approved by a parent, and agrees to a code of conduct.
  • Sessions are recorded for safety and quality. Recordings are stored securely, reviewed only by authorized staff, and deleted after a short period.
  • Tutors and students never message each other privately or contact each other outside the platform.
  • Anyone can report a concern at any time, and we follow up.

Cost

Always free. Axion STEM Tutors is run by volunteers.

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service for grades 2-6.

This site does not collect any information from children.
