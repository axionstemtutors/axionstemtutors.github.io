Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for parents and tutors.

Is Axion STEM Tutors really free?

Yes. Sessions are always free for families, and our tutors volunteer their time.

Which grades and subjects do you cover?

Grades 2-6, in math, science, and computer science.

How long is a session?

Each session lasts 80 minutes and takes place online over video.

Who are the tutors?

Trained teen volunteers, age 14 and older. Each is approved by a parent, completes training, and agrees to our code of conduct.

Are sessions one-on-one?

Usually, yes. Occasionally two students of a similar grade share a tutor, and we tell you ahead of time. You can choose one-on-one only, which may mean a longer wait.

Does my child need to show their face?

No. The camera is optional, and audio-only is always fine.

Can I watch a session?

Yes. Parents are welcome to sit in on any session.

Are sessions recorded?

Yes, for safety and quality. Recordings are stored securely, reviewed only by authorized staff, and deleted after a short period. We ask for your permission first.

What information do you collect about my child?

As little as possible. A parent creates the account, and we ask for permission before collecting anything about a child. You can ask to see or delete your child's information at any time.

What does my child need to join?

A computer or tablet with internet, and a quiet place to work. A pencil and paper help too.

When do sessions start?

We are still getting ready. Parents and teen tutors can join our interest list on the For parents and For tutors pages, and we will contact you first.

How can a teen become a tutor?

See the For tutors page. Teens 14 and older apply with a parent's approval, a reference, and an interview, then complete training.

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service for grades 2-6.

This site does not collect any information from children.
