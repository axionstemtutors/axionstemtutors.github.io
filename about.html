About us

Free STEM help for grades 2-6, taught by trained teen volunteers.

Our mission

Axion STEM Tutors gives every elementary student in grades 2-6 free, friendly help in math, science, and computer science. Strong early habits in these subjects open doors later, and no family should have to pay for that help.

How we do it

We pair students with trained teen volunteers for 80-minute online sessions. Teens learn by teaching, and younger students learn from someone close to their own age who remembers what it feels like to be stuck.

What we believe

  • Free for every family. Always.
  • Parents lead. Families give permission and can watch any session.
  • Safety comes first. Trained tutors, recorded sessions, and no private contact outside the platform.
  • Curiosity over speed. We care about understanding, not just right answers.

Where we are now

We are building our first group of tutors and getting ready to welcome families. We are also working toward registering as a nonprofit. Meet our leadership team.

Axion STEM Tutors is a free tutoring service for grades 2-6.
