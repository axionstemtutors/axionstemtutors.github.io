Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Axion STEM Tutors ("we") is a free tutoring service for grades 2-6. We keep what we collect to a minimum and never sell it.

Right now: this website

  • Interest and contact forms. If you fill one out, we receive the details you enter, such as your name and email, and the grade ranges and subjects you choose. Forms are delivered to us by FormSubmit, a third-party form service.
  • Children. This site does not ask children for any information. Our forms are for parents, guardians, and high school students in grades 9-12 (with a parent or guardian's contact details).
  • No ads or trackers. We do not use advertising or tracking tools on this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs such as visitors' IP addresses.

When sessions begin

Before we collect any information about a child, a parent or guardian will create an account and give consent. See our notice to parents. We will collect only what is needed to run sessions, which includes a child's first name or nickname, grade, subjects, and participation in sessions. Sessions are recorded under our recording policy.

How we use information

  • To reply to you and plan our program
  • To schedule and run sessions, keep them safe, and record volunteer hours
  • To report totals, such as hours given and students helped, with no individual details

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it except with service providers that help us run Axion (such as video, hosting, and email tools), or when required by law or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions only as long as we need them to respond and plan, and then delete them. Recording retention is described in our recording policy.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct, or delete your information, or your child's information, at any time. Use our contact form and choose "Privacy request."

Security

We limit who can access information, use strong passwords and two-factor authentication for our team, and choose service providers carefully.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here with a new date. Questions? Use our contact form.
